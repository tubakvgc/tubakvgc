### About me

I'm a cybersecurity professional and **Digital Forensics Engineering graduate** from **Fırat University (2024)**. My work and interests sit at the intersection of security operations, proactive threat detection, incident investigation, and practical security tooling.

I enjoy turning security research into **reproducible investigations, useful automation, and resources the community can learn from**.

### Areas of focus

- **Security operations & threat hunting:** SIEM-driven investigation, log analysis, alert triage, and detection workflows.
- **Threat intelligence:** Tracking emerging threats, investigating indicators of compromise (IOCs), and connecting intelligence to defensive operations.
- **Security engineering & automation:** Building Python-based tools that make analysis and vulnerability tracking more repeatable.
- **Vulnerability research:** Investigating web application weaknesses and documenting findings through technical proof-of-concepts.
- **Digital forensics & incident response:** Evidence analysis, investigation techniques, and practical DFIR workflows.
